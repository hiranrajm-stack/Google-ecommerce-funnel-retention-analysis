**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Product Leadership Team  
**SUBJECT:** E-Commerce Funnel & Retention Analysis (GA4 Cohorts: Nov 2020 – Jan 2021)  

### **Headline Finding**
Our 1.5% purchase conversion rate and 4% Week 1 retention—consistent across all eight cohorts—reveal critical, structural revenue leakage at product discovery, checkout completion, and post-visit re-engagement.

---

### **Funnel & Retention Diagnostics**
* **Merchandising Gap (85% View-to-Cart Drop-off):** Traffic is failing to convert into buyer intent. This indicates issues on product detail pages (PDPs), such as poor product imagery, uncompetitive pricing, weak value propositions, or lack of social proof.
* **Checkout Friction (85% Abandonment at Checkout):** High-intent shoppers are blocked at the final step. This points to UX and transactional friction, such as unexpected shipping costs, forced registration, or cumbersome payment methods.
* **Retention Deficit (4% Week 1 Return Rate):** The store functions as a one-off destination with virtually no customer stickiness, significantly eroding the ROI of customer acquisition.

---

### **Recommendations**
1. **Optimize PDP Merchandising:** A/B test enhanced product descriptions, customer reviews, clear inventory signals, and prominent add-to-cart CTAs to lift initial purchase intent.
2. **Streamline the Checkout Flow:** Remove purchase friction by introducing guest checkout, showing transparent upfront shipping costs, and enabling one-click digital wallets (e.g., Apple Pay, Google Pay).
3. **Launch Automated Lifecycle Triggers:** Deploy immediate browse- and cart-abandonment email/SMS sequences and targeted post-visit nurture campaigns to increase Week 1 return visits.
